# Working Group Formation Process

This document outlines the process for proposing and forming a new working group within the TeachLink Backend project.

## Proposing a Working Group

To propose a new working group, a contributor must submit a Pull Request to the Governance repository containing a proposal document. The proposal must outline:
- The purpose and goals of the working group.
- The initial members and the designated lead.
- The specific area of the project the group will focus on.

## Approval Requirement

The formation of a new working group requires approval from at least two core maintainers. This approval must be recorded on the proposal Pull Request before the working group is officially recognized.

## Initial Deliverables

Upon approval, the newly formed working group must provide the following initial deliverables within the first month:
- A charter document defining the scope, responsibilities, and decision-making process of the group.
- A roadmap outlining the group's planned activities and milestones for the upcoming quarter.
