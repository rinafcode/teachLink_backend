The mentorship program pairs an experienced contributor with a newcomer for a
fixed period, so that a first contribution is a supported one rather than a
guessing game. This document describes how pairs are made, what each side is
expected to do, and how the pairing is reviewed and closed.

## 1. How mentors and mentees are matched

- **The program is opt-in for both sides.** A mentor volunteers and a mentee
  asks to join; no one is assigned against their wishes.
- **Matching is by interest and capacity, not seniority alone.** A mentee states
  the area they want to work in (for example, the NestJS API layer, tests,
  documentation, or database work); a mentor with that experience and available
  time is paired with them.
- **Start from a good first issue.** The pair normally begins from an issue
  labelled as suitable for newcomers, following
  [`policies/GOOD_FIRST_ISSUE.md`](GOOD_FIRST_ISSUE.md) and
  [`policies/FIRST_TIME_CONTRIBUTOR.md`](FIRST_TIME_CONTRIBUTOR.md).
- **Kept small.** A mentor takes on at most one or two mentees at a time so the
  pairing stays real rather than nominal.
- **Coordination.** A maintainer keeps the pair list, the area, and the start
  date; the list lives with the project's contributor records.

## 2. Expectations

**The mentor:**

- makes the first contact and agrees a working rhythm with the mentee;
- explains the project's conventions and points at
  [`processes/ONBOARDING.md`](../processes/ONBOARDING.md) and
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md);
- reviews the mentee's work with the tone in
  [`community/REVIEW_ETIQUETTE.md`](REVIEW_ETIQUETTE.md), and lets the mentee do
  the work rather than taking it over;
- is responsive, and says when they will be unavailable;
- treats the mentee as a peer, not a task queue.

**The mentee:**

- drives their own work and asks questions rather than waiting silently;
- reads the contribution guide and tries the tooling before asking;
- takes on feedback and iterates;
- keeps the mentor informed of blockers;
- respects the mentor's time — batch questions, and use public channels where
  the answer helps others.

**Both** follow [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and use public
channels for technical discussion, so the answers benefit the wider community.

## 3. Duration and review

- **Default duration is one contribution cycle**, typically **6–8 weeks**,
  ending when the mentee's first change is merged or the period closes.
- **A short check-in at the midpoint** confirms the pairing is working; either
  side may ask to change mentor, area, or pace without it being a failure.
- **A closing review** records what the mentee worked on, what they learned, and
  whether they want to continue independently or take another pairing.
- **Renewal is optional.** A successful pairing may be renewed for a further
  cycle if both sides want it and the mentor has capacity.
- **A graduated mentee is a new contributor**, and progression afterward follows
  [`processes/PROMOTION_CRITERIA.md`](../processes/PROMOTION_CRITERIA.md).
- **Mentor recognition.** Mentoring is credited under
  [`RECOGNITION.md`](../RECOGNITION.md); mentors who sustain the program are
  noted in the maintainer records.

## 4. When it is not working

- Either side may end a pairing at any time by telling the programme
  coordinator; no reason is required.
- If a pairing raises a conduct concern, it is handled under
  [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), not within the pair.
- A mentor who repeatedly does not respond is offered support or replaced; the
  mentee is never left without a contact.
