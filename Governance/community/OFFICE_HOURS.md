Office hours are a recurring, open drop-in where contributors can ask a
maintainer questions in real time. They complement, and do not replace, the
asynchronous channels in
[`policies/COMMUNICATION_NORMS.md`](../policies/COMMUNICATION_NORMS.md).

## 1. Schedule and format

- **Cadence.** Office hours run **once every two weeks**, alternating between two
  time slots so that contributors in different regions can attend at least one
  per month. The exact times are published on the repository's community page and
  kept in step with
  [`processes/MEETING_CADENCE.md`](../processes/MEETING_CADENCE.md).
- **Duration.** 60 minutes, unless the host announces otherwise in advance.
- **Channel.** A public voice/video room announced with the session; a text
  channel is open in parallel for those who cannot join by voice.
- **Format.** An open floor with a loose agenda:
  1. a two-minute recap of open topics, drawn from submissions (§3);
  2. the bulk of the time on live questions;
  3. a close that records actions and links them to issues.
- **Recorded by default?** No. Sessions are not recorded unless every participant
  agrees; the written notes are the record either way.
- **Cancellation.** If no host is available, the session is cancelled on the
  community page at least 24 hours ahead.

## 2. Who hosts

- A **maintainer hosts** each session — see
  [`roles/MAINTAINER.md`](../roles/MAINTAINER.md) — and rotates the duty so the
  load is shared.
- A qualified contributor may host with a maintainer's agreement; the maintainer
  remains accountable for any decision made.
- The host is responsible for: publishing the session, keeping time, taking
  notes, and ensuring that any decision or action is captured in an issue.
- If a topic needs a decision rather than a discussion, the host routes it to the
  correct process (an issue, an RFC, or a vote) rather than deciding ad hoc. See
  [`processes/PROPOSAL_LIFECYCLE.md`](../processes/PROPOSAL_LIFECYCLE.md).

## 3. How topics are submitted

- **In advance, via an issue or the pinned thread.** Anyone may add a topic
  before the session; a topic should name the question, the relevant issue or PR,
  and whether it needs a decision or just discussion.
- **Live topics are welcome.** Time is reserved for questions that come up during
  the session.
- **Priority.** Topics with a linked issue and a clear question are taken first;
  open-ended design debates without a link may be deferred to a dedicated
  discussion, following
  [`policies/COMMUNICATION_NORMS.md`](../policies/COMMUNICATION_NORMS.md) §3.2.
- **No support-only sessions.** Office hours are for contributors and project
  work; end-user support stays in the community support channel.

## 4. After the session

- The host posts short notes within **2 business days**: topics covered,
  decisions, and action items with an owner and a due date.
- Action items become issues so they are tracked like any other work; a decision
  that changes a documented rule follows the normal change process.
- Sensitive matters (security, conduct, personal data) are **not** discussed in
  office hours; the host redirects them to the private channels in
  [`SECURITY_POLICY.md`](../SECURITY_POLICY.md) and
  [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
