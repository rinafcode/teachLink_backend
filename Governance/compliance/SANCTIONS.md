# Sanctions Compliance Policy

This policy sets the minimum sanctions-screening and escalation requirements
for project-controlled financial relationships and transfers. It is governance
guidance, not legal advice; applicable obligations depend on the parties,
activities, and jurisdictions involved.

## Scope

This policy applies to maintainers and the treasurer when they arrange or
approve community treasury payments, grants, rewards, sponsorships, vendor
relationships, or other project-controlled transfers of value.

It does not require screening every platform user or ordinary account activity
unless an applicable legal obligation or a documented risk assessment requires
it. It does not replace any stricter legal, contractual, or provider
requirements.

## Screening Requirement

Before establishing an in-scope relationship or authorizing a transfer, the
responsible maintainer or treasurer must screen the relevant individual and
organization against current official sanctions lists applicable to the
activity and jurisdictions involved. These may include lists maintained by
the United Nations, the United States (OFAC), the United Kingdom, and the
European Union, as applicable. Use authoritative sources and record which
sources and date were checked.

Repeat screening before later transfers when list changes, new information, or
the risk of the relationship makes a prior check unreliable. Screening must be
proportionate, use only the identifying information reasonably needed to
resolve a match, and be handled confidentially.

## Potential and Confirmed Matches

- **Potential match:** Do not treat a name-only or otherwise inconclusive match
  as confirmation. Pause the proposed onboarding or transfer while the match
  is reviewed; do not make accusations or disclose the screening result to the
  subject.
- **Confirmed match or other credible sanctions concern:** Do not begin or
  continue the proposed relationship or transfer unless and until qualified
  advice confirms it is permitted. Follow any applicable requirements to
  block, freeze, reject, or report the transaction or property. Do not move,
  return, or otherwise deal with potentially restricted property without
  direction from qualified counsel or the relevant authority.
- **No match:** Record the screening date, sources, reviewer, and outcome in
  the restricted project record for the relationship or transfer.

Screening records must be access-limited, retained only as long as needed for
legal, audit, and governance purposes, and must not include unnecessary
identity documents or sensitive personal information. Do not put personal
screening details in public issues, pull requests, or governance reports.

## Escalation

Anyone who identifies a potential match or other credible sanctions concern
must promptly pause the affected proposed activity and notify the treasurer
and lead maintainer through a private channel. If either is involved in the
matter or unavailable, notify another uninvolved maintainer. The lead
maintainer must ensure the concern is reviewed promptly by qualified legal or
compliance counsel before the project proceeds or makes a disclosure.

The reviewer must document the decision and its basis in a restricted record.
Where applicable law requires blocking, freezing, rejection, or reporting,
maintainers must follow counsel's direction and the relevant authority's
requirements without delay. Escalation must preserve confidentiality and must
not be used to evade a legal restriction or alert a subject in a way that
could compromise a required action.

## Ownership and Review

The maintainers own this policy. The treasurer is responsible for screening
in-scope treasury activity; maintainers are responsible for other in-scope
relationships they approve. Maintainers must review this policy at least
annually and whenever a material change in applicable obligations or project
activities warrants an earlier review. Changes are recorded in
`Governance/CHANGELOG.md`.
