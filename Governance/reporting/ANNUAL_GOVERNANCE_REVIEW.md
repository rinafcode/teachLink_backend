## 1. Scope of the annual review

The annual governance review covers:

- All existing governance policies and their relevance.
- Compliance with documented policies.
- Effectiveness of governance processes.
- Gaps in governance coverage.
- Updates needed to existing policies.

## 2. Participants

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Project lead | Approves the review plan and final report |
| Maintainer Team | Conducts the review and documents findings |
| Contributors | Provide feedback on governance effectiveness |

## 3. Outputs

The review produces:

- An annual governance review report published in `Governance/reporting/`.
- A list of policy updates and new policies to create.
- An action plan with owners and deadlines for each identified item.
