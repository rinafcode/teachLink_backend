## 1. When retrospectives happen

- **Sprint retrospectives**: Every 2 weeks, at the end of each sprint.
- **Release retrospectives**: After each release, within 5 business days.
- **Quarterly retrospectives**: At the end of each quarter.
- **Incident retrospectives**: Within 3 business days of any major incident.

## 2. Format

Each retrospective follows this format:

1. **What went well**: Positive outcomes and successes.
2. **What did not go well**: Challenges and areas for improvement.
3. **Action items**: Specific, owned, time-bound improvements.
4. **Follow-up**: Review of action items from the previous retrospective.

## 3. How actions are tracked

- Action items are recorded as GitHub issues with the `retrospective` label.
- Each action item has an owner and a due date.
- Action item status is reviewed at the next retrospective.
- Completed action items are closed; overdue items are escalated to the
  project lead.
