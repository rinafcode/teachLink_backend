## 1. Report sections

Each state-of-the-project report must include:

- **Executive summary**: High-level status and key achievements.
- **Health metrics**: Build pass rate, open issues, open PRs, and contributor activity.
- **Milestone progress**: Status of current milestones and roadmap items.
- **Risk and blockers**: Current risks and items blocking progress.
- **Community health**: New contributors, retention, and community engagement.
- **Next period priorities**: Planned focus areas for the next reporting period.

## 2. Cadence

Reports are published **quarterly**, within 10 business days after quarter end.

## 3. Publication location

Reports are published in the `Governance/reporting/` directory of the
repository and linked from the project's main communication channels.
