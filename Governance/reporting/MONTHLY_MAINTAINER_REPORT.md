## 1. Report fields

Each monthly maintainer report must include:

- **Merged PRs**: Count and summary of merged pull requests.
- **Open issues**: Count and priority breakdown of open issues.
- **Security advisories**: Any security issues addressed or pending.
- **CI/CD health**: Build pass rate and any failing jobs.
- **Contributor activity**: New contributors and active contributor count.
- **Blockers**: Items blocking progress and mitigation plans.

## 2. Cadence

Reports are published on the **first business day of each month** covering the
previous calendar month.

## 3. Audience

The report is shared with:

- All maintainers and contributors.
- Project stakeholders.
- Published in the repository's Governance/reporting/ directory.
