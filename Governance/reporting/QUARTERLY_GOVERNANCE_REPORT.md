## 1. Report contents

Each quarterly governance report must include:

- **Policy changes**: New policies adopted and existing policies updated.
- **Compliance status**: Summary of compliance with active policies.
- **Audit results**: Outcomes of internal and external audits.
- **Incident summary**: Governance-related incidents and resolutions.
- **Action items**: Open action items from the previous quarter and their status.
- **Upcoming changes**: Planned governance changes for the next quarter.

## 2. Publication cadence

Reports are published within **10 business days** after the end of each
calendar quarter (Q1: January, Q2: April, Q3: July, Q4: October).

## 3. Owner

The Maintainer Team is responsible for preparing and publishing the report.
The project lead approves the final report before publication.
