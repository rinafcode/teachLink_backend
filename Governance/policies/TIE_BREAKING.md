# Tie-Breaking Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the procedure for resolving ties during decision-making processes within the TeachLink Backend project.

## Resolving Ties
In the event of a tied vote among the maintainers or project members on a proposal, technical decision, or governance matter, the tie will be resolved by a casting vote.

## Casting Vote
The **Lead Maintainer** holds the casting vote. Their decision will break the tie and finalize the outcome.

## Fallback
If the Lead Maintainer is unavailable or must recuse themselves due to a conflict of interest, the casting vote will fall to the **next most senior active maintainer** based on project tenure. If no such person is available, the decision will be deferred until a casting voter is available, or an external arbitrator agreed upon by the core team will be consulted.
